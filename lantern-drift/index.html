Lantern Drift

A quiet logic puzzle for iPhone.

How to play

Place lanterns on the lake so that every row, every column and every colored pond holds the same number of lanterns — one, or two in ★★ levels. Lanterns can never touch each other, not even diagonally.

Tap a cell once to mark it with ×, twice to place a lantern, and a third time to clear it. Drag across cells to mark many × at once. Every puzzle has exactly one solution and can be solved by logic alone.
