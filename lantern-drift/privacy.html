Privacy Policy

Lantern Drift · Last updated October 28, 2026

Data Not Collected

Lantern Drift is a single-player puzzle game. We built it to be private by design.

Data we collect

None. The app does not collect, store on any server, share or sell any personal information. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party tracking SDKs.

Data stored on your device

Your puzzle progress, best times, daily-puzzle streak and settings (sound, haptics, mistake highlighting) are saved only on your device so you can continue where you left off. This data never leaves your device. Deleting the app removes it.

Network access

The game works fully offline. It does not make network requests during play.

Children

Because no data is collected from anyone, no data is collected from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
