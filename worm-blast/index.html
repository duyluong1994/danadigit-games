Worm Blast

Turn-based worm warfare for iPhone. Aim, charge and BOOM — 39 crazy weapons, endless stages, creepy crawlers and bosses.

Coming soon to the App Store Offline · No ads · No data collected

How to play

Each turn you control one worm. Blow the enemy critters off the map before they do it to you.

  • Joystick left or right to crawl, up to jump. The ▲ ▼ buttons set your angle.
  • Hold FIRE to charge power, release to shoot. Or drag back from your worm like a slingshot.
  • Tap the weapon button to open the arsenal and pick one of 39 weapons.
  • Every blast digs a crater. Wind pushes rockets, so check the WIND bar before you fire.

Adventure

An endless trail of stages across meadows, deserts, snow, lava and the moon. Win to earn gold and unlock weapons, spend gold in the shop, and face a boss at the end of every world.

Watch out for crawlers: bugs that creep toward your worms and explode. Falling rocks and supply crates keep every stage different.

Daily quests and a login streak give bonus gold. There are no energy bars or timers between stages.

Questions

Where is my progress saved?

Your stages, stars, gold, weapons and settings are stored only on your iPhone. Deleting the app removes them.

Does it need an internet connection?

No. Worm Blast works fully offline.

Are there ads or in-app purchases?

No. Gold is earned only by playing.

How do I turn the sound off?

Tap the speaker button on the main menu or in the pause menu, or use the Sound row in Settings.

Can two people play on one phone?

Yes. Choose 2 Players on the main menu and pass the phone between turns.
