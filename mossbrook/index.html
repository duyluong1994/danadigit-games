Mossbrook

A cozy forest village for iPhone. Grow, bake and craft for the woodland folk, and clear brambles as the seasons turn.

Coming soon to the App Store Offline · No ads · No data collected

How to play

Bring a forgotten forest village back to life with the woodland folk.

  • Plant crops in your fields. They grow in real time, even while you are away.
  • Workshops turn crops into flour, pies, jam, soup and cozy crafts.
  • Neighbours post requests. Deliver them for acorns and golden leaves.
  • Golden leaves fill the season bar. Each new season brings new crops and brambles.

Brambles

Brambles block the forest path. Clear one in a match-3 level to reveal new plots, workshops and cottages for new neighbours.

Swap two neighbouring pieces to line up three or more. Line up four for a gust of wind, an L or T for a chestnut burst, and five for a firefly that clears a whole kind.

There are no lives or timers in brambles: play as many as you like.

Questions

Where is my progress saved?

Your village, pantry and settings are stored only on your iPhone. Deleting the app removes them.

Does it need an internet connection?

No. Mossbrook works fully offline. Crops and workshops keep running on your phone's clock.

Are there ads or in-app purchases?

No. Acorns are earned only by playing.

Does it work on iPhone Duo?

Yes. The layout adapts when you fold or unfold the screen.
